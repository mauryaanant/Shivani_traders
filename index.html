About Us

Bridging Indian Markets
to the World

Shivani Traders, headquartered in Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh, has grown into one of the region's most reliable FMCG distributors. As an authorized agency for Coca-Cola, Tops, Tata Consumer Products, and Mother Dairy, we serve hundreds of retail outlets across the region with timely and quality service.

Beyond domestic distribution, we have expanded our reach to international markets through our premium rice export division — supplying the finest Basmati and Non-Basmati rice varieties to buyers across the globe.
